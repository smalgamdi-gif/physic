## 4. Exam style
1. **Format:**
   - Every question is multiple choice A–D, one correct answer, one mark each (0.5 in the LMS assignment).
   - No written solutions are asked for.
2. **Two kinds of questions, in this order:**
   - CLO **1.1** comes first: SI units, prefixes, definitions, scalar vs vector, the three laws of Newton written as equations, the vector‑product formula.
   - CLO **2.1** comes second: one‑step calculations.
3. **Calculations are short:**
   - One formula and one substitution, with round numbers (3‑4‑5 triangle, 108 km/h, 20 °C, 60 m/s in 3 s, 10+20+30 Ω).
   - A figure accompanies most of them.
4. **The key word is underlined:** displacement, density, force, equivalent resistance, voltage difference, luminous intensity, horizontal component.
5. **Typical distractors:**
   - multiplying instead of dividing (52800 vs 13600);
   - adding magnitudes instead of using Pythagoras (7 vs 5);
   - distance vs displacement;
   - the initial velocity instead of zero at maximum height;
   - the wrong component (sin vs cos).
6. **Topics seen:**
   - Midterm: chapters 1–4.
   - LMS assignment 1: chapters 1–4.
   - Final: all chapters, from units to light (Kelvin, reflection, circuits).
